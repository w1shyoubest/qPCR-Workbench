qPCR Plate Designer

Plan each plate and keep its record in one place: the design, the plate map well by well, and the protocol (reagents, cycling, log). After the run, open its file in the Reader to see the design next to the data and write your results. Everything stays in this browser; save a project file to keep or share a copy.
